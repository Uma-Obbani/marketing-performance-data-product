# Marketing Performance Dashboard Wireframe

## Objective
Provide marketing managers with a single, trusted view of campaign performance.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. KPI Summary
- Total marketing spend
- Total conversions
- Attributed revenue
- Overall ROAS

### 2. Campaign Performance
A comparison table showing:
- Campaign name
- Marketing channel
- Region
- Marketing spend
- Conversions
- Attributed revenue
- ROAS

### 3. Filters
- Reporting period
- Marketing channel
- Region
- Campaign

### 4. Data Quality Information
- Data validation status
- Number of affected records
- Last successful refresh
- Warnings for unreliable data

## User Experience Requirements
- Simple and easy-to-understand layout.
- Consistent KPI definitions.
- Clear currency and reporting period.
- Users can filter and compare campaigns.
- Unreliable metrics must be clearly identified.

## Future Enhancements
- Campaign trend analysis.
- Budget allocation recommendations.
- Automated alerts.
- Advanced attribution analysis.

## Product Owner Note
This wireframe represents a proposed MVP experience. Final requirements and layout should be validated with Marketing Managers and stakeholders before development.
