# User Stories — Marketing Performance Data Product

## 1. Purpose

Translate the business problem and product vision into user-centred requirements with testable acceptance criteria.

This is a simulated portfolio project. Stories and criteria are proposed until validated with stakeholders.

## 2. User Story US-001 — View Consistent KPI Definitions

**As a** marketing manager,  
**I want** to access documented definitions for spend, conversions, attributed revenue and ROAS,  
**so that** I can interpret campaign performance consistently.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Each in-scope KPI has a documented formula and reporting period.
- Attribution rules are documented where applicable.
- An authoritative source and business approver are identified.
- Approval status is visible.
- Unapproved KPIs are not presented as validated for campaign comparison.

## 3. User Story US-002 — Compare Campaign Performance

**As a** marketing manager responsible for weekly budget allocation,  
**I want** to compare spend, conversions, attributed revenue and ROAS across selected campaigns,  
**so that** I can identify performance differences and make informed budget decisions.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Users can select an agreed reporting period.
- Users can compare the agreed KPIs for campaigns in the MVP scope.
- All compared figures use consistent reporting and attribution rules.
- Unavailable or unvalidated figures are clearly identified.
- A test using known sample data produces the expected results.

## 4. User Story US-003 — Identify Data-Quality Issues

**As a** marketing manager,  
**I want** to see when a KPI has failed its agreed validation checks,  
**so that** I do not base budget decisions on untrusted figures.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Failed checks generate a visible status or warning.
- The affected KPI and reporting period can be identified.
- The issue is recorded for investigation by the responsible team.
- Unvalidated figures are not presented as approved results.

## 5. User Story US-004 — Compare Campaigns by Region

**As a** marketing manager,  
**I want** to compare campaign performance by region,  
**so that** I can understand regional differences and evaluate where budget changes may be appropriate.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Regional comparison is available for supported campaigns and regions.
- Region definitions and mappings are documented.
- Missing or unmapped regional data is identified.
- Users can compare the agreed KPIs for the same reporting period.
- Pilot feedback and usage evidence are collected to evaluate the feature.

## 6. User Story US-005 — Reduce Manual Reporting

**As a** marketing manager,  
**I want** to access campaign performance data without manually combining multiple reports,  
**so that** I can spend less time preparing reports and more time making decisions.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Users can access the agreed campaign metrics in one place.
- The product displays the reporting period and data freshness.
- A pilot measures report preparation time before and after introduction.
- Users can report missing information that still forces manual exports.

## 7. Dependencies

- US-001 depends on stakeholder agreement and approval of KPI definitions.
- US-002 depends on the required source data being available and validated.
- US-003 depends on agreed data-quality checks and issue ownership.
- US-004 depends on reliable regional mappings and sufficient data coverage.
- US-005 depends on delivery of the required metrics and user workflow.

## 8. Next Steps

Validate the stories with representative users and delivery teams, estimate their effort, prioritize the backlog and agree on the MVP release scope.
