# Marketing Performance Data Product — Product Roadmap

## Product Goal
Help marketing managers make faster, evidence-based campaign and budget decisions using trusted performance data.

## Phase 1 — MVP (Weeks 1–4)
**Goal: Establish trusted campaign reporting.**

Deliverables:
- Agree on KPI definitions with Marketing and Finance.
- Validate campaign data quality.
- Build marketing KPI overview.
- Enable campaign comparison.
- Add reporting period, channel and region filters.
- Conduct stakeholder testing.

Success measures:
- KPI definitions approved.
- Critical data-quality checks pass.
- Marketing managers can compare campaigns in one place.

## Phase 2 — Adoption and Improvements (Weeks 5–8)
**Goal: Improve usability and adoption.**

Deliverables:
- Collect feedback from pilot users.
- Improve dashboard usability.
- Add regional performance analysis.
- Reduce manual Excel reporting.
- Monitor product usage and reporting preparation time.

Success measures:
- Increased weekly active usage.
- Reduced manual reporting time.
- Positive feedback from marketing managers.

## Phase 3 — Advanced Capabilities (Weeks 9–12)
**Goal: Support better marketing investment decisions.**

Deliverables:
- Campaign performance trend analysis.
- Budget allocation insights.
- Automated performance alerts.
- Explore advanced attribution requirements.

Success measures:
- More timely campaign decisions.
- Improved visibility into campaign performance.
- Evidence of improved marketing efficiency.

## Dependencies
- Access to reliable campaign data.
- Marketing and Finance stakeholder availability.
- Agreement on attribution rules.
- Data engineering and BI development capacity.

## Risks
- Inconsistent source data.
- Conflicting KPI definitions.
- Low user adoption.
- Delays in data integration.

## Product Owner Responsibilities
- Maintain roadmap priorities.
- Validate stakeholder needs.
- Align delivery with business outcomes.
- Review progress and reprioritize based on evidence.

## Important Note
This is an illustrative 12-week roadmap for a portfolio exercise. Actual timelines depend on discovery, technical estimates, team capacity and stakeholder agreement.
