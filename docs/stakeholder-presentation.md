# Marketing Performance Data Product
## Stakeholder Presentation

### Slide 1 — Business Problem
Marketing managers currently combine campaign reports manually using Excel.
Different reporting sources and inconsistent KPI definitions make campaign comparisons difficult.

### Slide 2 — Product Vision
Create a trusted marketing performance product that enables faster, evidence-based campaign and budget decisions.

### Slide 3 — Target Users
- CMO: Business performance and marketing investment visibility.
- Marketing Managers: Campaign comparison and optimization.
- Finance: Revenue and financial KPI validation.
- Data Analysts: Consistent reporting and data quality.

### Slide 4 — Proposed Solution
A centralized marketing performance dashboard providing:
- Marketing spend
- Conversions
- Attributed revenue
- ROAS
- Campaign comparison
- Region and channel filters
- Data quality status

### Slide 5 — MVP Priorities
1. Agree on KPI definitions.
2. Validate source data.
3. Deliver KPI overview.
4. Enable campaign comparison.
5. Pilot with selected marketing managers.

### Slide 6 — Product Roadmap
- Weeks 1–4: MVP and trusted reporting.
- Weeks 5–8: Adoption and usability improvements.
- Weeks 9–12: Advanced insights and alerts.

### Slide 7 — Success Measurement
- Reduction in manual reporting preparation time.
- Weekly active marketing managers.
- Critical data-quality validation results.
- Faster campaign decision-making.
- Long-term profitable revenue improvement.

### Slide 8 — Risks and Dependencies
- Inconsistent source data.
- Conflicting KPI definitions.
- Attribution disagreements.
- Engineering capacity.
- Stakeholder availability.

### Slide 9 — Decisions Required
Request stakeholder agreement on:
- KPI definitions and ownership.
- Attribution model.
- MVP scope.
- Pilot users.
- Success measurement baseline.

### Slide 10 — Next Steps
- Conduct stakeholder discovery.
- Confirm requirements.
- Validate data sources.
- Estimate delivery effort with engineering.
- Agree on MVP release plan.
