# Marketing Performance Data Product

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the end-to-end lifecycle of a Data Product Owner initiative for a fictional e-commerce company.

The company spends €1 million per month on digital marketing but struggles to compare campaign performance because of inconsistent metrics, manual reporting, and limited confidence in its data.

The goal is to design a data product that helps marketing managers make faster, evidence-based budget allocation decisions.

**Project type:** Simulated portfolio case study  
**Role:** Data Product Owner  
**Domain:** Marketing Analytics and Data Products

## 2. Business Problem

Marketing managers lack a consistent and trusted view of campaign performance across marketing channels.

Current challenges include:

- Inconsistent conversion and revenue figures across platforms.
- Time-consuming manual reporting in Excel.
- Delayed budget allocation decisions.
- Limited visibility into campaign efficiency and profitability.

These challenges may lead to ineffective marketing budget allocation and missed opportunities to improve business performance.

## 3. Business Objective

Help the company increase profitable revenue by 15% within six months without increasing its €1 million monthly marketing budget.

This is a proposed business target for the simulated case study, not an achieved result.

## 4. Target Users

- Marketing managers responsible for campaign budgets.
- Chief Marketing Officer (CMO).
- Finance stakeholders validating revenue and profitability.
- Data analysts and data engineers supporting reporting and data quality.

## 5. Proposed Product

A Marketing Performance Data Product that provides trusted, comparable campaign performance metrics, including:

- Marketing spend.
- Conversions.
- Attributed revenue.
- Return on ad spend (ROAS).

The initial release will focus on a limited number of marketing channels and agreed KPI definitions.

## 6. MVP Scope

The minimum viable product will focus on:

1. Documenting and approving KPI definitions.
2. Validating the relevant marketing data sources.
3. Comparing campaign performance across selected channels.
4. Identifying data-quality discrepancies.
5. Testing the product with marketing managers.

Advanced attribution, AI-powered recommendations, and automated budget changes are outside the initial scope.

## 7. Success Metrics

Proposed measures include:

- Weekly active usage among target marketing managers.
- Reduction in manual reporting time.
- Compliance with agreed data-quality criteria.
- Reduction in unnecessary Excel exports.
- Change in marketing contribution profit and profitable revenue.

Baselines and targets will be validated before evaluating product performance.

## 8. Project Roadmap

1. Business problem discovery.
2. Product vision and stakeholder mapping.
3. KPI definitions and requirements.
4. User stories and acceptance criteria.
5. MVP scope and backlog prioritization.
6. Data validation and prototype development.
7. User testing and release decisions.
8. Adoption and business-value measurement.
